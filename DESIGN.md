## Our Story & Mission

**Brand Story:** Started as an educational placement in 2013, Kenneth discovered that many foreigners shared a simple dream: to provide their family and children with a better living environment. Seeing the struggles faced by these working adults in getting Permanent Residence, Kenneth & Co. was born with a simple purpose: to simplify and provide transparency to the process. We firmly believe that every individual deserves an equal opportunity to build a brighter future through the elusive benefits here in Singapore.

**Vision:** To be the top of mind, trusted consultancy that sincerely serves deserving individuals in acquiring a permanent residency for a better future.

**Mission:** We are on a mission to impact the lives of our clients with our personalised and honest solutions. We consider it a privilege to elevate your chance of success by overcoming the struggles in your application. As competition continues to soar, we are committed to overcoming these challenges together with you.

**Tagline:** *Your Future, Our Mission*

## Our Logo

Representing the pathways people take to achieve success, the Kenneth & Co. logo expands and shows the different paths the customers can take. The foundation being a rounded square, it makes use of negative space to create a vision of the K pathway, or the Kenneth & Co. path to success.

### Logo Variations
* **Horizontal Logo** (Preferred for left alignment on visuals/documents)
* **Vertical Logo** * **Icon** (The 'K' in a rounded square)

### Tagline Usage
* **With Tagline:** Recommended for official documents, posters, and important company materials. It emphasizes company values.
* **Without Tagline:** Recommended for common social media, electronic digital marketing materials, and when the logo is scaled down to a size where the tagline is no longer readable.

### Clear Space & Scaling
* **Clear Space:** Use the triangle shape from the 'K' icon (X) as a minimum buffer guide. The surrounding area should fit 1 triangle shape on all sides to allow the logo to breathe.
* **Scaling:** Scale should be a minimum of 25% up to 35% of the document width. 
    * A6 (105 x 148mm): 26mm minimum width
    * A5 (148 x 210mm): 37mm minimum width
    * A4 (210 x 297mm): 52mm minimum width
    * A3 (297 x 420mm): 74mm minimum width
    * A2 (420 x 594mm): 105mm minimum width

### Background Variations
* **Light Backgrounds:** Use the Original (Blue/Black), All Black, or Black/Blue versions.
* **Dark Backgrounds:** It is mandatory to use the All White version for legibility.
* **Image Backgrounds:** Ensure there are no interfering highlights/shadows. Subtly manipulate image areas to increase legibility if needed.

### Logo Misuse (Don'ts)
* Do not add a drop shadow (unless the whole icon is merged, e.g., for physical signage).
* Do not stretch, compress, warp, or flip.
* Do not blur (unless it is part of the image's depth perspective).
* Do not recolor.
* Do not rotate or shear (unless part of a realistic image perspective).
* Do not outline.
* Do not duplicate or stack.

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
The foundational colors convey a corporate, professional, and reliable tone. These should be the dominant colors in any visual design.

* **Kenneth & Co. Blue** (Corporate foundation)
    * HEX: `#365462`
    * RGB: 54, 84, 98
    * CMYK: 82, 58, 46, 27
* **Kenneth & Co. Black**
    * HEX: `#231F20`
    * RGB: 35, 31, 32
    * CMYK: 0, 0, 0, 100
* **Kenneth & Co. White**
    * HEX: `#FFFFFF`
    * RGB: 255, 255, 255
    * CMYK: 0, 0, 0, 0

### Secondary Colors
Secondary colors provide vibrancy and a lighter, more human emotion.

* **Ivory** (Reliability and humanly nature)
    * HEX: `#EDF5E5`
    * RGB: 237, 245, 229
    * CMYK: 6, 0, 11, 0
* **Illuminating Emerald** (Reliability)
    * HEX: `#359683`
    * RGB: 53, 150, 131
    * CMYK: 77, 21, 56, 3
* **Fuzzy Wuzzy** (Warmth, connection to clients as family)
    * HEX: `#D7676D`
    * RGB: 215, 103, 109
    * CMYK: 12, 73, 49, 0
* **Sweet Brown** (Warm and true)
    * HEX: `#A33226`
    * RGB: 163, 50, 38
    * CMYK: 24, 92, 96, 19

*(Note: Tints and shades of Kenneth & Co. Blue and Ivory can be used for visual depth, but should be limited to 5 variations.)*

### Color Application Guidelines
* **Backgrounds:** Prioritize lighter tones (White and Ivory). Use dark backgrounds only when necessary. Avoid using Illuminating Emerald and Sweet Brown as background colors.
* **Text:** Black and Blue for light backgrounds. White and Ivory for dark backgrounds. Emerald and Fuzzy Wuzzy can be used for emphasis. Do NOT use Illuminating Emerald, Fuzzy Wuzzy, or Sweet Brown for main body text.
* **Accents:** Prioritize Sweet Brown, Fuzzy Wuzzy, and Illuminating Emerald for accent visuals and CTA buttons. Use Ivory sparingly for accents, ensuring adequate contrast.

## Typography

Kenneth & Co. typography aims for three keywords: Clean, Professional, and Sincere.

### Logo Typefaces
* **Brand Name:** Palanquin Dark
* **Tagline:** Catamaran

### Web and Visual Typefaces
* **Poppins** (Portrays boldness and familiarity)
    * *Usage:* Titles and Headings. 
    * *Rule:* Keep minimal. Do not use for body text or heavy text content. Avoid using 2+ Poppins titles in one visual.
* **Cormorant Garamond** (Shows sophistication, class, and professionalism)
    * *Usage:* Subtitles, Headings, Subheadings, and Button text.
    * *Rule:* Do not use for heavy body text.
* **Roboto** (Brings friendliness, approachability, and sincerity)
    * *Usage:* Body text and text-heavy content (utilize different font weights).
    * *Rule:* Avoid using for main titles, headings, or stylized quotes.

### Typographic Application Rules
* **Scaling:** Recommended to use the golden ratio (x 1.618) when scaling up text hierarchy.
* **Spacing:** Play with kerning and line height to ensure text is highly legible. Strictly avoid compressed kerning and line-heights.

## Visual Elements

### 1. Imagery (Preferred Medium)
* **Style:** People-centric. Show emotion, results, happiness of individuals/families, professionalism of agents, and sincerity in helping others.
* **Quality:** Must be well-lit and high resolution.
* **Don'ts:** Avoid negative emotions, poorly lit, low-resolution, or blurry images.

### 2. Illustrations
* **Style:** People-centric, depicting the same positive values as the imagery.
* **Quality:** High-resolution vector style.
* **Don'ts:** Do not use abstract styles, convey negative emotions, or use blurry illustrations.

### 3. Symbols
* **Style:** Outlined style only. Must always use brand colors.
* **Application:** Use minimally. Contrast colors depending on the background. Focus colors (accents) can be added to symbols if appropriate.
* **Don'ts:** Do not use heavy-stroked or filled-style symbols.
